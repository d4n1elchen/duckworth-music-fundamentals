Ch 4: Compound Meter (6/8 vs. 3/4), Syncopation & Radar

When each big beat divides naturally into three equal parts, you’re in Compound Meter. Spin the geometric radar to see 6/8 vs. 3/4!

🔵 6/8 = Compound Duple (two ♩. beats = 3+3) 🟢 3/4 = Simple Triple (three ♩ beats = 2+2+2) 🗣️ Compound count: 1 - la - le - 2 - la - le

🛸 1. Geometric Rhythm Radar: 3/4 Triangle vs. 6/8 Axis

🥁 2. Compound 6-Step Drum Machine (1 - la - le) & Syncopation

6/8, 6/4 → 2 big beats 9/8, 9/4 → 3 big beats 12/8, 12/4 → 4 big beats

⚡ 3. Ties, Syncopation & Borrowed Divisions (Triplet 3 & Duplet 2)

🔗 Tie vs. Slur

Tie joins the same pitch (sustained); Slur connects different pitches (legato).

⚡ Syncopation

Accents or longer notes placed on weak beats / offbeats (♪ ♩ ♪) for forward groove.

🔀 Triplet 3 & Duplet 2

Simple beat borrowed in 3 = Triplet 3; compound beat borrowed in 2 = Duplet 2.
